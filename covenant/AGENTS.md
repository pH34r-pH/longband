# Covenant map

[`voluntary-privacy-norm.md`](voluntary-privacy-norm.md) is participant-visible social-contract text delivered after admission. Receipt is required for ordinary participation; agreement, ideology, loyalty, or silence is not. Keep the norm editable through public contribution and preserve its distinction from cryptographic enforcement.

Changes should identify the affected invariant and update the admission/protocol documentation if delivery or receipt semantics change. Preserve prior research/history rather than rewriting it in place.
